# PRD: Personal Portfolio + RYT KEY TECH

## 1. Purpose
A website that wins clients for RYT KEY TECH. It presents Annor Sampson as the person behind the business and proves skill with real, working systems built for real businesses.

## 2. Target users
- **Primary:** Small and medium business owners in Ghana (shops, phone/repair shops, retailers, lenders, service businesses) who need a website, POS/inventory system, dashboard or custom web app.
- **Secondary:** Referrals and partners checking credibility before recommending you.
- **Device:** Mostly mobile phones, sometimes slow data. Site must be fast and mobile-first.

## 3. Problems it solves
- Business owners don't know if you can deliver → show a real production system (GENXCHOICE POS).
- They don't understand tech terms → explain services as business results (track stock, stop theft, see daily profit).
- They want to talk fast → one-tap WhatsApp contact everywhere.

## 4. MVP features (version 1)
1. **Hero section:** your name, "Founder, RYT KEY TECH", one-line value statement, WhatsApp button, "See my work" button.
2. **Services:** Business websites, Inventory/POS systems, Data dashboards, Custom web apps. Each with a plain-language benefit.
3. **Featured project: GENXCHOICE POS (case study)**
   - The problem the shop had
   - What was built: inventory, sales/POS, repairs, purchases, expenses, reports, admin/worker roles, approvals, audit logs
   - Results for the business
   - Screenshots (no real customer data)
   - Tech used: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase, Vercel
4. **About:** short story — data science degree, running GENX CHOICE ENTERPRISE, building systems you use in your own business.
5. **How I work:** 4 steps — Talk → Plan → Build → Support.
6. **Contact:**
   - Floating WhatsApp button on every screen, pre-filled message
   - Contact form (name, business name, phone, what you need) that sends to email
7. **Footer:** RK logo, WhatsApp, email, copyright.
